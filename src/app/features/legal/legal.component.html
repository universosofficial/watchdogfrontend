3. Bilateral Agreements & Version Locking

Deal terms proposed within the platform become binding once digitally accepted by both parties:

  • Agreement Locking: Digital acceptance locks the agreed item description, condition disclosures, delivery expectations, and price (Version 1.0).

Both parties agree to document fulfillment transparently:

  • Delivery & Handover Proof: Sellers attach handover photos, serial numbers, or courier tracking information before or during delivery.

When a buyer records a payment on the platform:
